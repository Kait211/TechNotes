# Scenario 1:

You’re doing systems administration work for Network Funtime Company. Evaluate their current IT infrastructure needs and limitations, then provide at least five process improvements and rationale behind those improvements. Write a 200-400 word process review for this consultation. Remember, there’s no right or wrong answer, but make sure to provide your reasoning.

Software Company:

Network Funtime Company is a small company that builds open-source software. The company is made up software engineers, a few designers, one person in Human Resources (HR), and a small sales team. Altogether, there are 100 employees. They recently hired you as a system administrator to come in and become their IT department.

When a new person is hired on, the HR person purchases a laptop for them to do their work. The HR representative is unfamiliar with what type of hardware is out there; if a new employee requests a laptop, the HR person will purchase the cheapest option for a laptop online. Because of this, almost everyone has a different laptop model. The company doesn’t have too much revenue to spend, so they don’t order laptops until someone gets hired at the company. This leads to a few days of wait time from when someone starts to when they can actually work on a laptop.

The company doesn’t label their computers with anything, so if a computer is missing or stolen, there’s no way to audit it. There’s no inventory system to keep track of what’s currently in the fleet.

Once a computer is purchased, the HR person hands it to the new employee to set up. Software engineers that use Linux have to find a USB drive and add their preferred distribution to the laptop. Anytime someone needs something from HR -- whether it’s office related or tech related -- they email the HR representative directly.

When a new employee gets a machine, they’re given logins to use cloud services. They get a personal orientation with HR to make sure they can login. This requires the HR person to block off a few hours for every new employee. If an employee forgets the login to their machine, they have no way to retrieve a password and they have to reimage their machine. Employees don’t have a strict password requirement to set for their computers.

The company currently has many of their services in the cloud, such as email, word processors, spreadsheet applications, etc. They also use the application, Slack, for instant communication.

### Answer:
There are several problems here that can be fixed or mitigated by an IT professional, for instance the issue of Laptop Purchasing and Hardware. HR does not buy employees a standardized laptop instead opting for the cheapest laptop and this is an issue. Different laptops have different issues therefore any issues would be more difficult to solve and learn from. Therefore, as an IT professional I would research the best and affordable Laptop then procure these for new employees before deploying them out, also I would advise to always have a few extras of these laptops on hand so when someone new is hired they can start work right away. Next, I would set up an Inventory system for the laptops and hardware that are sent out to employees. I would put numbers on all laptops and make an excel spread sheet that lists what employees have what tech so that we know at all times when something is missing. Next, I would separate HR and Tech, if there an HR problem employees would go to HR but for Tech issues there would have to be a new line of communication for a person that knows and gets paid for Tech issues.  After this I would set up a standardized computer setup process, I would use LDAP as a centralized employee directory which would keep track of employees’ names, email addresses, passwords, groups, organizational info, and their access permissions. Instead of creating separate usernames and passwords for every application, I will use LDAP to create accounts in one centralized directory saving hours setting up each laptop individually. I can also use LDAP to help employees who forgot their passwords to recover their passwords, I would also require all employee passwords to be at least 8 characters long, have 2 caps, and at least 2 unique symbols. Lastly, I would use google services such as google docs as the cloud services that employees will use. Also, for Slack, employees would have to authenticate who they are before they are allowed to communicate for security that way sensitive company data does not leak.

# Scenario 2:

You’re doing systems administration work for W.D. Widgets. Evaluate their current IT infrastructure needs and limitations, then provide at least five process improvements and rationale behind those improvements. Please write a 200-400 word process review for this consultation. Remember, there’s no right or wrong answer, but make sure to provide your reasoning.

Sales Company:

W.D. Widgets is a small company that sells widgets. They’re mostly made up of salespeople who work with lots of clients. You’ve taken over as the sole IT person for this company of 80-100 people.

When HR tells you to provision a machine for a new employee, you order the hardware directly from a business vendor. You keep one or two machines in stock, in case of emergency. The users receive a username that you generate for them. You then give them an orientation on how to login when they start. You currently manage all of your machines using Windows Active Directory. The company uses only Windows computers. When a new computer is provisioned, you have to install lots of sales-specific applications manually onto every machine. This takes a few hours of your time for each machine. When someone has an IT-related request, they email you directly to help them.

Almost all software is kept in-house, meaning that you’re responsible for the email server, local machine software, and instant messenger. None of the company’s services are kept on the cloud.

Customer data is stored on a single file server. When a new salesperson starts, you also map this file server onto their local machine, so that they can access it like a directory. Whoever creates a folder on this server owns that folder and everything in it. There are no backups to this critical customer data. If a user deletes something, it may be lost for everyone.

The company generates a lot of revenue and is rapidly growing. They’re expecting to hire hundreds of new employees in the next year or so, and you may not be able to scale your operations at the pace you’re working.

### Answer:
The solutions I would present for W.D Widgets is first I would set up a document with links that explains to new employees the sales-specific applications that they would need to install into their computer and what they do. This would be done during orientation, and I would try to do this for a whole group so that several computers could be set up at once saving time. I would recommend using LDAP to set up new employee’s accounts/logins and have them create their own passwords that are required to be unique and hard to hack. I would also require all employees to change their passwords once a month if they do not do so they will be locked out of the system. By using LDAP I could save hours of time and be able to keep up with hundreds of new employees that will be coming. Also, with LDAP I could set file permissions and access control to all data and folders I would do this because it would protect data from being accessed by bad actors. I would suggest a ticketing system such as Trello to list problems, what issues are in progress, and document what has been solved. I would also recommend an IT team made up of several experienced individuals so that issues within tech that come up can be solved in an more efficient matter and that I the sole IT person will not become overwhelmed by the company’s IT issues. I would also use Google cloud instead of the current company services so that the cloud can handle the email server, local software, documentation, and more. That why the cloud can handle those systems for me and any issues that come with it. Next, I would set up a full backup and have that on site and somewhere off site so that if there is a fire for instance the company would not lose all customer and company data. After the full backup I would include Differential backups and Regular incremental backup so that we would not have to do full backups everyday saving time and money, Full backups would still occur but only once every 6 months.

Non-profit Company:

Dewgood is a small, local non-profit company of 50 employees. They hired you as the sole IT person in the company. The HR person tells you when they need a new computer for an employee. Currently, computers are purchased directly in a physical store on the day that an employee is hired. This is due to budget reasons, as they can’t keep extra stock in the store.

The company has a single server with multiple services on it, a file server, and email. They don’t currently have a messaging system in place. When a new employee is hired, you have to do an orientation with them for login. You’re also responsible for installing all the software they need on their machine, and mapping the file server to their computer. The computers are managed through Windows Active Directory. When an employee leaves, they’re currently not disabled in the directory service.

The company uses an open-source ticketing system to handle all internal requests as well as external non-profit requests. But the ticketing system is confusing and difficult to use, so lots of the employees reach out to you directly to figure out how to do things. In fact, so many things are difficult to find that employees typically ask around when they have a question.

There are nightly backups in place of the file server. You store this information on a disk backup and take it home with you everyday to keep it safe in case something happens onsite. There’s also a small company website that’s hosted on the single server at the company. This website is a single html page that explains the mission of the company and provides contact information. The website has gone down many times, and no one knows what to do when it happens.

### Answer:
For Dewgood what I would do is first I would set up a standardized procurement process, when an employee is hired onto the team they will start on a Monday in the mean time we would purchase and set up their computer before their first day so that when they start they can do work right away. I would make sure to do research and have all employees have the same type of computer that is reliable and affordable. Next, I would improve the company's server infrastructure by implementing Google Cloud services. I would use Google Cloud to host the company's servers and manage file storage, while using Google Workspace to handle corporate email, calendars, and employee collaboration and I would also recommend Slack as an instant messaging platform. Next, I will write out documentation on how to install all the required software and what that software does. Then during orientation, I would have all incoming workers install their applications needed which would save time compared to me installing it on their computers one at a time. I would use LDAP to manage employees accounts, this would allow me to manage not only the setup process of an employee such as email, username, and login. But also allow me to have a proper employee offboarding process meaning I will be able to relinquish an ex-employee’s access to data and information once they leave. I would also fix the company’s current ticketing system I would recommend using Trello instead and allow employees to make tickets for current issues so that everyone and especially IT can look at the current issues and see the solutions being worked on to fix these issues. Next, I would solve the backup and recovery problem instead of one disk that I take home every night I would make two disks one there and one that would stay safe in my home which will be backup every night. I would also save all company data on an online server as well preventing a disaster such as a fire from destroying all important data. Now for the website I would have a team fix it and write post-mortem if how we fixed the issue. This way once the website is fixed it will be easier to fix it the next time it goes down also doing so could prevent it going down in the future
