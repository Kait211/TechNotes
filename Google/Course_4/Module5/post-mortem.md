We create a **post-mortem** after an incident, an outage, or some event when something goes wrong, or at the end of a project to analyze how it went 

The purpose is to learn from an event not to shame 

# What should a post-mortem include...
### Brief Summary
- What the incident was
- How long it lasted
- what the impact was
- how it was fixed

### Detailed Timeline
- Have correct dates, times, and timezones

### Root Cause 
- Explain what lead to the issue 
- What can be learned 

### Resolution and Recovery Efforts
- How the problem was solved
- Should include date, times, and timezones

### Actions to Avoid Same Scenarios 
- List of specific actions that should be taken to avoid the same scenario from happening again
- ​This should include any actions or ​efforts aimed at improving the response handling too
- Steps to reduce response ​timeline enhancements to monitoring would help. 
- lookout for things like improvements to monitoring systems


One aspect of post-mortem that are often overlooked are what went well. Its good to list what went well such as an alert sounded that allowed us to to find the issue quickly before too much damage occurred
